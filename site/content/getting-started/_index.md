Ticketmaster (`tm`) is an interactive coding agent for your terminal. You chat with an agent live,
then hand follow-up work to background workers as **tickets**, review their submissions, and keep
shipping.

- [Install](@/getting-started/install.md) the `tm` binary.
- Follow the [Quickstart](@/getting-started/quickstart.md) to open your first chat.
- Configure a model [provider](@/reference/providers.md).

Source: [github.com/alliecatowo/ticket-master](https://github.com/alliecatowo/ticket-master).
